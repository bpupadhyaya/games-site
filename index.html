$2.99

Flick, pocket, cover the queen

Carrom is the flick-and-pocket board game played across South Asia, in homes, clubs and tournaments. Slide your striker along the baseline, pull back to aim and let go: coins slide, rebound off the cushions and drop into the corner pockets. Learn in eight hands-on lessons, play the computer at four levels or a friend on one phone, and try a new trick shot every day. A polished plywood board, glossy coins and a dotted aim guide that shows where each shot will go. Large text, reduced motion and left-handed layout included. No ads, works offline.

  • No ads
  • Works offline
  • One-time price
How to play
  1. New here? Tap Learn to play: eight short lessons where you make every shot yourself.
  2. DRAG the striker sideways along your baseline to place it. It turns red if it touches a coin.
  3. DRAG BACK from the striker to aim. The dotted line shows the shot; a longer pull is a harder flick. RELEASE to flick.
  4. Pocket a coin of your colour and you shoot again. Pocket nothing, or the other colour, and the turn passes.

  8. Keyboard: Left and Right slide, A and D aim, Up and Down set power, Space flicks. Hint shows a good shot.
