Golden Sling

Slingshot Skill

A hand-carved slingshot, a golden field, and birds raiding the crop. Pull back, aim and release to scare them off before the harvest is gone. Every bird behaves differently, the wind rises as you advance, and every miss sends the flock scattering.

How to play: Drag back from the slingshot to aim — the dotted arc shows where the stone starts to fly — then release. Hit birds to score and build combos, never hit the protected owl, and reach each level's quota before your stones or the crop run out. The full game is free in the apps, with every level, Endless mode and a new Daily Hunt each day.

Word Game

Timed Vocabulary Drill

A target word appears and three candidate words drift across the screen. Tap the correct synonym or antonym before it drifts away — over 300 graduate-level words, with a full review of every answer after each session.

How to play: Pick Synonym or Antonym, then tap Play for a 90-second session. Tap the one word that matches the target. When time is up, review each word you answered — what you tapped, the correct answer and its meaning.

Crown Fields

Logic Puzzle

Place one crown in every row, column and coloured region — no two crowns may touch, even diagonally. Every puzzle is generated fresh with exactly one solution.

How to play: Tap a cell to cycle empty → cross → crown. Placing a crown auto-crosses cells it would make illegal. Fill every region correctly to solve the puzzle.

Sure Sweep

No-Guess Minesweeper

Classic mine-clearing with one guarantee: every board can be fully solved by logic alone — no lucky guesses required.

How to play: Tap to reveal a cell. Flag cells you're sure are mines. Tap a revealed number when its flags match it to auto-clear the safe cells around it. Clear the board to win.

Big Card Solitaire

Large-Print Solitaire

Klondike redrawn for comfort: oversized high-contrast cards, tap-to-move instead of dragging, no timers or flashing effects — and every deal is checked so it can actually be won.

How to play: Tap a card, then tap where you want it to go. Build the four foundations up by suit from Ace to King. Use the "what can I do?" hint if you're ever unsure of a legal move.
