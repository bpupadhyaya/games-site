Big Card Solitaire

Huge cards, tap to play, every deal winnable.

About this game

Big Card Solitaire is Klondike redrawn for comfort: oversized high-contrast cards, tap-to-move instead of dragging, and no timers or flashing effects. Every deal is checked so it can actually be won. A built-in "what can I do?" hint highlights your legal moves whenever you're unsure.

How to play

  1. Tap a card to select it, then tap where you want it to go.
  2. Build the four foundation piles up by suit, from Ace to King.
  3. Stack tableau cards down in alternating colours.
  4. Tap the stock pile to draw a new card when you're stuck.
  5. Tap "what can I do?" any time to see every legal move highlighted.
